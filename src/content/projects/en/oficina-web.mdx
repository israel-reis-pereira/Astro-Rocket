## What it is

**Oficina.Web** is a web system developed to support the operational workflow management of an automotive repair shop.

The project was born with a core principle that sets it apart from simply adding technologies to a system: first, validate the experience, navigation, and workflow; then, introduce persistence, business rules, and abstractions only when a genuine need arises.

Currently, the project is being developed as an **incremental MVP**, utilizing **.NET 10, ASP.NET Core, and Blazor Web App with Interactive Server**.

## The problem the project aims to solve

The system was designed to map out the service workflow of a repair shop, starting from the arrival of the customer and vehicle all the way to service completion.

The planned operational workflow is:

```text
Registration
   ↓
Quote / Estimate
   ↓
Approval
   ↓
50% Down Payment
   ↓
Parts Ordering
   ↓
Service Execution
   ↓
Remaining Payment
   ↓
Vehicle Delivery
```
