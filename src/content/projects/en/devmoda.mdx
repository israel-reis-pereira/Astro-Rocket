## What it is

**DEVmoda** was developed as part of building my portfolio during my Information Systems bachelor's degree.

The project started as an interface proposal for an online store and evolved into a lab for studying front-end development, responsiveness, JavaScript, API consumption, and experimental integration with artificial intelligence.

Rather than representing a production-ready store, the project logs a significant milestone in my progression with interface development and the integration of different front-end resources.

## Project evolution

The development progressed through distinct stages:

```text
Landing page
      ↓
Product pages
      ↓
Product details
      ↓
JavaScript interactions
      ↓
External data consumption
      ↓
Experimental AI integration
      ↓
Refactoring
```
